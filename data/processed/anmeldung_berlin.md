# Anmeldung in Berlin

## Where

Any Bürgeramt in Berlin can register you, regardless of the district (Bezirk) you live in. Appointments are booked centrally on service.berlin.de under "Anmeldung einer Wohnung". New slots are released continuously, often early in the morning; refreshing the booking page several times a day is the usual way to find a slot within the two-week window.

## Online registration

Berlin offers the digital Anmeldung ("Elektronische Wohnsitzanmeldung") for people who have a German ID card, eID card for EU citizens or a residence permit with activated online ID function and the AusweisApp. The Wohnungsgeberbestätigung is uploaded during the online process and the Meldebescheinigung is provided digitally.

## Documents

Bring your passport, the Wohnungsgeberbestätigung signed by the landlord (Berlin provides an official form that landlords are used to), and the completed Anmeldeformular. For spouses and children bring the certificates as described in the general Anmeldung guide.

## After registration

Berlin's Landesamt für Einwanderung (LEA, the immigration office) uses the registered address for all correspondence. Your Finanzamt is determined by the district of your address (for example Finanzamt Mitte/Tiergarten for parts of Mitte).
