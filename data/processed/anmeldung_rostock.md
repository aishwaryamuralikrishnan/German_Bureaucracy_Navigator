# Rostock: where to go for registration, residence permits and taxes

## Anmeldung: the Ortsämter

In Rostock the registration office (Meldebehörde) is called Ortsamt, not Bürgeramt. The city has five Ortsämter and you can use any of them regardless of your district:

- Ortsamt Mitte, Neuer Markt 1a, 18055 Rostock (city centre)
- Ortsamt West, Goerdelerstraße 53, 18069 Rostock (Reutershagen)
- Ortsamt Ost, Jawaharlal-Nehru-Straße 33, 18147 Rostock (Toitenwinkel)
- Ortsamt Nordwest 1, Albrecht-Tischbein-Straße 48, 18109 Rostock (Groß Klein)
- Ortsamt Nordwest 2, Warnowallee 30, 18107 Rostock (Lütten Klein)

An appointment is mandatory and is booked online through the city portal rathaus.rostock.de ("Wohnung anmelden" / Ortsämter page, https://rathaus.rostock.de/de/rathaus/stadtverwaltung/ortsaemter/250807). If no slot is free, check again around 9:00 in the morning when cancelled appointments are released. All family members being registered must attend in person. Registration is free and must be done within two weeks of moving in (§ 17 BMG).

Documents: passport or ID card of every person, the Wohnungsgeberbestätigung from the landlord, and for families the marriage certificate and children's birth certificates with certified German translations. The University of Rostock's International Welcome Center publishes an English step-by-step guide for students and researchers.

## Residence permits: the Migrationsamt

Rostock's immigration office is the Migrationsamt der Hanse- und Universitätsstadt Rostock, Neuer Markt 3, 18055 Rostock (next to the Rathaus). Visits are only possible with a prior online appointment: https://rathaus.rostock.de/OnlineTermine/select2?md=9. Opening hours: Monday 9:00–12:00, Tuesday 9:00–12:00 and 13:30–18:00, Thursday 9:00–12:00 and 13:30–16:00. Contact: 0381 381-2251, migrationsamt@rostock.de. It handles residence permits (Aufenthaltserlaubnis) including the conversion of national visas, the EU Blue Card, settlement permits, work authorisations and integration-course certificates.

## Taxes: Finanzamt Rostock

Finanzamt Rostock, Möllner Straße 13, 18109 Rostock. Phone (0385) 588-44 000. Central information and reception desk: Monday 9:00–12:00, Tuesday 9:00–12:00 and 14:00–17:00, Thursday 9:00–12:00, Friday 9:00–12:00. Requests such as a change of tax class (Steuerklassenwechsel) can be submitted here or online via ELSTER.

## Public holidays

Rostock is in Mecklenburg-Vorpommern. In addition to the national holidays, Reformation Day (31 October) is a public holiday, and since 2023 International Women's Day (8 March) as well; offices are closed on these days and statutory deadlines that fall on them move to the next working day.
