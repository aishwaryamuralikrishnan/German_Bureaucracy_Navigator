# Family reunification (Familiennachzug), §§ 27–36 AufenthG

## Who can join

Spouses or registered partners and unmarried minor children of a person who holds a residence title in Germany can apply to join them. Parents of skilled workers who first received their title after 1 March 2024 may also join under certain conditions (§ 36a AufenthG), provided the skilled worker can cover living costs and both parents apply together.

## Requirements for the spouse of a skilled worker or Blue Card holder

- Both spouses are at least 18 years old and the marriage is recognised in Germany (marriage certificate with apostille or legalisation and certified translation).
- The sponsor holds a valid residence title and has sufficient living space (Wohnraum) and a secured livelihood (income covering the household without social benefits).
- German language requirement: spouses of EU Blue Card holders, skilled workers under §§ 18a/18b, researchers and highly qualified persons are exempt from proving basic German (A1) before entry. Other spouses normally need A1.
- Health insurance cover from arrival.

## Children

Minor children can join both parents or the parent with sole custody. Children who are 16 or older and join only one parent may need to show German language skills or a positive integration prognosis unless a Blue Card holder is the sponsor.

## Procedure

The family member applies for a national visa at the German embassy or consulate in the country of residence. Processing usually takes several weeks to months; appointments should be booked early. After entry, the family registers (Anmeldung) and applies for the residence permit at the Ausländerbehörde within the validity of the visa, normally 90 days.

## Rights after arrival

Spouses of Blue Card holders and skilled workers receive immediate, unrestricted access to employment and self-employment. The family residence permit is tied to the sponsor's permit; after three years of marriage in Germany the spouse can obtain an independent residence right.

## Documents typically required

Passports, marriage and birth certificates with legalisation/apostille and translations, proof of the sponsor's residence title and income (employment contract, payslips), rental contract with size of the flat, health insurance confirmation, biometric photos, completed application forms and, where required, the A1 language certificate.
