# Bank account (Girokonto) and SCHUFA

## Why you need a German account early

Salary, rent, health insurance and most subscriptions in Germany run through SEPA transfers and direct debits (Lastschrift). Employers can pay to any EU IBAN, but landlords and utilities often expect a German account. A bank account is also needed to receive the deposit refund and to pay the Rundfunkbeitrag.

## Right to a basic account (Basiskonto)

Under the Payment Accounts Act (Zahlungskontengesetz, ZKG) every person legally residing in the EU, including asylum seekers and people without a fixed address, has the right to a basic payment account at any bank that offers consumer accounts. The bank may not refuse because of a poor SCHUFA score. A Basiskonto allows deposits, withdrawals, transfers and direct debits but no overdraft.

## Documents

Passport or ID card; for third-country nationals usually the visa or residence title; the Meldebescheinigung (some online banks accept an address abroad or a hotel address initially, traditional banks want the registration); a Steuer-ID once you have it (banks must report it, but most open the account without it and ask later). Identity verification is done in a branch, via PostIdent at a post office, or by video call.

## Types of banks

- Traditional banks with branches (Sparkasse, Volksbank, Deutsche Bank, Commerzbank): personal service, higher fees, cash deposits easy.
- Direct banks (ING, DKB, comdirect, Consorsbank): usually free accounts with a salary inflow, good apps, English partly available.
- Fintechs (N26, Revolut, bunq, Vivid): fastest onboarding with a passport and video call, English interface; useful as a first account in the first weeks.

## SCHUFA

SCHUFA is the largest German credit reference agency. It records your accounts, credit cards, loans, mobile contracts and payment defaults and computes a score that landlords, lenders and phone providers use. A new arrival has no negative entries but also no history; this is normal and not a bad score. You are entitled to one free data copy per year (Datenkopie nach Art. 15 DSGVO) and can order a paid SCHUFA-BonitätsAuskunft to show landlords. Opening several accounts in a short time or applying for multiple credit lines can lower the score temporarily.
