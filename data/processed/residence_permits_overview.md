# Residence titles for working in Germany

## Main titles for skilled workers

- EU Blue Card (§ 18g AufenthG): academics with a job offer above the salary threshold.
- Residence permit for skilled workers with academic training (§ 18b AufenthG): for graduates whose job does not meet the Blue Card salary threshold; no fixed minimum salary, but the job must match the qualification and the employment conditions must be comparable to those of German employees.
- Residence permit for skilled workers with vocational training (§ 18a AufenthG): for people with a recognised vocational qualification.
- Residence permit for professionally experienced workers (§ 19c Abs. 2 AufenthG): for people with at least two years of professional experience and a state-recognised qualification from their home country, with a minimum salary requirement.
- Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte, § 20a AufenthG): a points-based title to come to Germany to look for a job for up to one year.
- Job-seeker visa for academics (§ 20 AufenthG) and residence permit for research (§ 18d AufenthG).

## Fiktionsbescheinigung

If you apply for an extension or a new title before your current title expires, your stay remains lawful while the application is processed (§ 81 Abs. 4 AufenthG). The Ausländerbehörde issues a Fiktionsbescheinigung as proof. It normally allows you to keep working under the conditions of the previous title. Applying after expiry can mean losing this protection, so always apply before the expiry date — authorities recommend at least eight weeks in advance because appointment waiting times are long.

## Extensions

Extensions are requested at the Ausländerbehörde responsible for your registered address. Typical documents: passport, current residence title, Meldebescheinigung, employment contract and recent payslips, proof of health insurance, proof of accommodation (rental contract), and the completed application form. For the Blue Card, the salary must still meet the threshold valid in the year of the extension.

## Permanent settlement permit (Niederlassungserlaubnis)

Skilled workers can usually apply after four years of holding a skilled-worker title (§ 18c AufenthG), or after three years for graduates of German universities. Blue Card holders qualify after 27 months, or 21 months with German at level B1. Requirements include secured livelihood, pension contributions for the required period, sufficient living space, basic knowledge of the legal and social order (usually via the "Leben in Deutschland" test) and German language skills (usually B1; A1 in some cases for Blue Card holders after 27 months).

## Notification duties

Report a change of address, employer (in the first year of a Blue Card), marital status, or loss of employment to the Ausländerbehörde promptly. A new address is reported via the Anmeldung, but the residence title card itself should be updated if the address is printed on it.
