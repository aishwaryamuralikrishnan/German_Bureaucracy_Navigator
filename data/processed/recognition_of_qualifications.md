# Recognition of foreign qualifications

## Regulated vs non-regulated professions

For regulated professions (doctors, nurses, pharmacists, teachers at state schools, lawyers, engineers using the title "Ingenieur" in some states, architects) you need formal recognition (Anerkennung) or a licence before you may work. For non-regulated professions (most jobs in IT, business, research, marketing) no formal recognition is needed to work, but it may be required for a visa or residence title and helps employers judge your degree.

## University degrees: anabin and ZAB

The anabin database of the Standing Conference of the Ministers of Education lists foreign universities and degrees. If your university is rated "H+" and your degree type is listed as comparable ("entspricht" or "gleichwertig"), the degree is generally accepted for the EU Blue Card and skilled-worker permits without further paperwork.

If your university or degree is not in anabin, or is rated "H+/-", you apply to the Central Office for Foreign Education (Zentralstelle für ausländisches Bildungswesen, ZAB) for a Statement of Comparability (Zeugnisbewertung). The application is online, the fee is EUR 200 for the first statement, and processing takes about three months (faster for skilled-worker visa applicants). Documents: degree certificate, transcript, proof of identity, and translations where the documents are not in German or English.

## Vocational qualifications

Vocational qualifications are assessed by the competent body for the profession (for example IHK FOSA for commercial and industrial occupations, the Handwerkskammer for crafts). The procedure checks equivalence with the German reference occupation and may result in full recognition, partial recognition with compensatory measures, or rejection. Fees range from about EUR 100 to 600. The recognition partnership (Anerkennungspartnerschaft) introduced in 2024 allows starting work in Germany while the procedure runs.

## Regulated professions in healthcare

Doctors need the Approbation from the state health authority (for example Landesamt für Gesundheit und Soziales in Berlin). Requirements include equivalence of the degree (or a knowledge test, Kenntnisprüfung), German at level B2 plus a medical language test at C1 (Fachsprachprüfung), a health certificate and a certificate of good conduct. Nurses need recognition by the state authority and German at B2.

## Where to get help

The portal anerkennung-in-deutschland.de has a "Recognition Finder" that identifies the competent authority for your profession and location. The hotline "Working and Living in Germany" (+49 30 1815-1111) advises in German and English. Local IQ Network (Integration durch Qualifizierung) advice centres help with applications free of charge.
