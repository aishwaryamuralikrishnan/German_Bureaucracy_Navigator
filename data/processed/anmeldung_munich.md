# Anmeldung in München

## Where

Registration is handled by the Kreisverwaltungsreferat (KVR) at its Bürgerbüros. The central office is at Ruppertstraße 19; there are further Bürgerbüros in Pasing, Orleansplatz (Haidhausen), Leonrodstraße (Neuhausen), Forstenrieder Allee and Riesenfeldstraße (Milbertshofen). Appointments are booked through the city's online appointment system; walk-in service is very limited.

## Documents

Passport or ID card, the Wohnungsgeberbestätigung, and the Anmeldeformular. Munich's form can be pre-filled online and printed or shown on a phone. The KVR also handles the Ausländerbehörde for Munich, so newcomers often visit the same building for the residence permit later.

## Practical notes

Munich is strict about the two-week deadline in principle but accepts that appointments may only be available later; keep a screenshot of the earliest bookable appointment as evidence. Registration is free.
