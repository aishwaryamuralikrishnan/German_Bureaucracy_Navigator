# Checklists for newcomers

## Timeline for the first weeks

1. Before or on arrival: choose a health insurance fund and confirm cover starts on your first working day; open a first bank account (a fintech account works within a day).
2. Week 1–2: find a home, get the Wohnungsgeberbestätigung, book and complete the Anmeldung (deadline: 14 days after moving in).
3. Week 2–4: Steuer-ID arrives by post; give it and the insurer's details to your employer; open or switch to your main bank account with the Meldebescheinigung.
4. Before the visa expires (within 90 days for most national visas): book the Ausländerbehörde appointment and apply for the residence title (for example the EU Blue Card).
5. Within 6 months: convert a non-EU driving licence if you plan to drive.
6. Ongoing: register for the Rundfunkbeitrag when the letter arrives, sign up for a German course, and keep every Bescheid in a folder.

## Checklist: Anmeldung

- Passport / ID for every family member
- Wohnungsgeberbestätigung signed by the landlord (§ 19 BMG)
- Completed Anmeldeformular
- Marriage certificate and children's birth certificates with translations (families)
- Appointment confirmation

## Checklist: EU Blue Card application at the Ausländerbehörde

- Valid passport and current visa or residence title
- Biometric passport photo
- Completed application form (Antrag auf Erteilung eines Aufenthaltstitels)
- Employment contract or binding job offer (at least six months) showing gross salary above the threshold
- "Erklärung zum Beschäftigungsverhältnis" completed by the employer
- Degree certificate plus anabin printout or ZAB Statement of Comparability
- Proof of health insurance
- Meldebescheinigung
- For regulated professions: professional licence
- Fee (up to EUR 100 for the first issue; less for extensions)

## Checklist: residence permit extension

- Passport and current residence title
- Meldebescheinigung and rental contract
- Employment contract and the last three payslips
- Proof of health insurance
- Completed extension application form
- Passport photo

## Checklist: family reunification (spouse)

- Passports of both spouses
- Marriage certificate with apostille/legalisation and certified German translation
- Sponsor's residence title, employment contract and payslips
- Rental contract showing the size of the flat
- Health insurance confirmation for the spouse
- A1 German certificate if not exempt
- Visa application forms and biometric photos

## Checklist: converting a driving licence

- Passport and Meldebescheinigung
- Original foreign licence and certified translation
- Biometric photo
- Eye test certificate (Sehtest)
- First-aid course certificate (if tests are required)
- Proof of date of residence in Germany
