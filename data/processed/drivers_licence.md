# Driving licence (Führerschein) for newcomers

## EU and EEA licences

A licence from an EU or EEA state remains valid in Germany without conversion. You may voluntarily exchange it for a German one; after it expires you must renew it in Germany.

## Licences from other countries

A licence from a non-EU country is valid for six months from the date you establish residence in Germany (the Anmeldung date counts). A German translation or international driving permit is required alongside the licence unless it is in German or the country is exempt. After six months you may no longer drive on the foreign licence and must convert it (umschreiben) at the driving licence office (Führerscheinstelle / Fahrerlaubnisbehörde). If you can prove you will stay less than twelve months, the six months can be extended by up to another six months on application.

## Conversion rules by country (Anlage 11 FeV)

Annex 11 of the Driving Licence Regulation (Fahrerlaubnis-Verordnung) lists countries whose licences are converted with reduced requirements:

- No tests required (only the paperwork): for example Switzerland, Japan, South Korea, Israel, Singapore, Australia (most states), New Zealand, Canada (most provinces), and a number of US states (the list of states is specific and should be checked).
- Theory test only or practical test only: some countries and some US states.
- Full theory and practical test: all countries not listed in Annex 11, which currently includes for example India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nigeria, Egypt, Turkey, Brazil, Mexico and China. In this case you do not need to take driving lessons but you must register with a driving school to be admitted to the tests.

## Documents for conversion

Passport and Meldebescheinigung, the original foreign licence with a certified translation (ADAC or a sworn translator), a biometric photo, an eye test certificate (Sehtest), a first-aid course certificate (Erste-Hilfe-Kurs) where a test is required, and proof of the date you took up residence. Some offices ask for proof that the licence was obtained while you lived in the issuing country. Processing takes several weeks; the German licence is a card valid for 15 years.

## Practical tips

Apply for the conversion well before the six months end, because the appointment and processing time can exceed the validity window. Driving on an expired foreign licence is treated as driving without a licence (Fahren ohne Fahrerlaubnis), a criminal offence.
