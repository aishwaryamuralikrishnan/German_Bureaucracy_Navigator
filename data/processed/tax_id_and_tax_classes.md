# Steuer-ID and tax classes

## Tax identification number (Steueridentifikationsnummer, Steuer-ID)

The Steuer-ID is an 11-digit number issued once for life by the Federal Central Tax Office (Bundeszentralamt für Steuern, BZSt). After your first Anmeldung in Germany it is sent automatically by post to your registered address, normally within two to four weeks. Your employer needs it for payroll; without it you are taxed provisionally in tax class VI (the highest deductions), which is corrected once the number arrives.

If the letter does not arrive, you can request the number again from the BZSt using the online form; it is sent by post only, never by email or phone, for data-protection reasons. The Steuer-ID is different from the Steuernummer, which the local Finanzamt assigns for tax returns or self-employment.

## Wage tax classes (Lohnsteuerklassen)

Wage tax is deducted monthly by the employer based on the electronic wage-tax deduction features (ELStAM). The tax class determines how much is withheld; the final tax liability is settled in the annual tax return.

- Class I: single, divorced, widowed (after the year following the death), or married with a spouse living permanently abroad.
- Class II: single parents entitled to the relief amount for single parents (Entlastungsbetrag für Alleinerziehende).
- Class III: married or in a registered partnership, if the other spouse is in class V or has no income; lowest deductions.
- Class IV: married couples where both earn; the default when both spouses live in Germany. Optionally with a factor (Faktorverfahren) that distributes the tax fairly.
- Class V: the spouse of a class III earner; highest deductions among the married classes.
- Class VI: for a second or further job, or when no Steuer-ID is available; no basic allowance.

Important: if your spouse still lives abroad, you are treated as class I unless the spouse lives in the EU/EEA, in which case class III may be possible on application.

## How your tax class is assigned

You do not choose your tax class when you arrive; it is derived from the data recorded at the Anmeldung (marital status, children, religion) and stored in the ELStAM database that your employer retrieves with your Steuer-ID. Single people automatically receive class I; married couples who both live in Germany automatically receive IV/IV; a single parent must apply for class II. Your current tax class is printed on every payslip (Lohnabrechnung) under "StKl" and can also be seen in your ELSTER account under "Meine ELStAM".

## Changing your tax class (Steuerklassenwechsel)

Who can change: only married couples and registered partners can switch between the combinations IV/IV, III/V and IV/IV with factor. Single people cannot choose a different class, except single parents applying for class II. The choice only affects how much wage tax is withheld each month; the final tax is identical after the annual return, so III/V mainly helps cash flow when incomes differ strongly (and it makes the annual tax return compulsory).

How to apply:

1. Fill in the form "Antrag auf Steuerklassenwechsel bei Ehegatten/Lebenspartnern". It is available online at ELSTER (Mein ELSTER → Formulare → Lohnsteuer → Antrag auf Steuerklassenwechsel) and as a PDF from the Federal Ministry of Finance forms centre (formulare-bfinv.de); paper copies are available at the Finanzamt.
2. Both spouses sign (the ELSTER version can be submitted with one certificate if the other spouse consents).
3. Submit it to the Finanzamt responsible for your registered address, online via ELSTER or by post / in person. No fee is charged.
4. The new class applies from the beginning of the month after the application is received; it cannot be applied retroactively for earlier months. Since 2020 the class can be changed several times per year, not just once.

Documents: Steuer-IDs of both spouses, the completed form; no marriage certificate is needed if the marriage is already recorded at the registration office. If the marriage took place abroad and is not yet recorded, submit the certified marriage certificate with translation to the Bürgeramt/Ortsamt first so that the marital status appears in ELStAM.

Related changes handled the same way: applying for class II as a single parent ("Antrag auf Lohnsteuer-Ermäßigung", section Entlastungsbetrag), choosing the factor method (Faktorverfahren) for IV/IV, and correcting a wrong class VI after a missing Steuer-ID (the employer corrects it automatically once the number is entered).

## Church tax (Kirchensteuer)

If you declare membership of a church that levies church tax at the Anmeldung, 8 % (Bavaria and Baden-Württemberg) or 9 % (other states) of your income tax is withheld as church tax. Leaving the church (Kirchenaustritt) is done at the Standesamt or Amtsgericht depending on the state, for a fee.

## Annual tax return (Einkommensteuererklärung)

Employees often are not obliged to file a return but usually benefit from doing so (relocation costs for a job move, work equipment, commuting, double household). Returns are filed via ELSTER or tax software; the deadline for mandatory returns is 31 July of the following year (later if a tax adviser files).
